Physics used

Light transport: milk scatters strongly (fat globules + casein micelles), so light diffuses. μs′ ≈ 7.5·fat%·(λ/600)−0.4 + 4·casein%·(λ/600)−2.2 cm⁻¹. Absorption μa = water + riboflavin (450 nm) + haemoglobin (542/577 nm, blood in mastitis).

Reflectance: Kubelka–Munk slab over a black back wall: K=2μa, S=0.75μs′, R=1/(a+b·coth(bSd)), a=1+K/S, b=√(a²−1), d=6 mm.

Signal: counts = 60 + G·LED(λ)·(R·Tw²+stray)·t, shot noise √N, 16-bit clip at 65535. Tw² = 0.927 (two glass–air surfaces, 3.5 % each); baffles cut stray ×0.12.

Flow: Re=ρvDh/μ, ΔP=12μLQ/(wh³(1−0.63h/w)), wall shear γ̇=6Q/(wh²), milk 1030 kg/m³ and about 2 mPa·s at 37 °C. Tracers run in slow motion.

Photons: illustrative Monte Carlo with an enlarged mean free path; survival per step is a′40 so absorption stays correct. Yield is photons that return inside a bore.

Illustrative model for design intuition, not a calibrated instrument. Thresholds must be calibrated against lab reference milk. Optical constants are typical literature-range values.
